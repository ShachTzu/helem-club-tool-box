---
description: "A protected page rendering a draft's full version history: a merged revision and approval timeline alongside a field-level diff viewer."
labels: ['editorial', 'history', 'revisions', 'rtl']
---

The `VersionHistory` page is served at `/library/draft/:id/history`, and is protected for writers,
moderators and admins. It renders a right-hand timeline merging the draft's revisions and approval
trail events, and a left-hand diff viewer comparing two selected versions — defaulting to the two
latest revisions. Selecting any two revisions on the timeline updates the comparison. A "שחזר גרסה
זו" button opens a Hebrew confirmation modal explaining that restoring creates a brand new revision
without deleting anything.

On mobile, the timeline and the diff viewer stack vertically, one below the other.

### Full history with five revisions

```tsx
<VersionHistory
  mockUser={{
    id: `writer-1`,
    email: `writer@helem.club`,
    displayName: `נועה כותבת`,
    role: `writer`,
    provider: `email`,
    createdAt: new Date(`2024-02-10T08:00:00.000Z`).toISOString(),
  }}
  mockDraft={{
    id: `draft-history-1`,
    contentType: `post`,
    title: `מדריך התחלה לחברי קהילה חדשים`,
    payload: {},
    domains: [`כללי`],
    status: `in_review`,
    authorId: `writer-1`,
    authorName: `נועה כותבת`,
    currentVersion: 5,
    createdAt: new Date(`2024-01-01T09:00:00.000Z`).toISOString(),
    updatedAt: new Date(`2024-05-15T12:30:00.000Z`).toISOString(),
  }}
/>
```

### A draft with a single revision

When a draft has only one revision, the page shows the timeline entry alone and a hint explaining
that at least two revisions are required to render a comparison.

```tsx
<VersionHistory
  mockDraft={{
    id: `draft-history-single`,
    contentType: `post`,
    title: `רשומה חדשה שזה עתה נוצרה`,
    payload: {},
    domains: [],
    status: `draft`,
    authorId: `writer-1`,
    authorName: `נועה כותבת`,
    currentVersion: 1,
    createdAt: new Date(`2024-06-01T09:00:00.000Z`).toISOString(),
    updatedAt: new Date(`2024-06-01T09:00:00.000Z`).toISOString(),
  }}
  mockRevisions={[]}
  mockApprovals={[]}
/>
```
